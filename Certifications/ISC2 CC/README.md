---

## 📘 About the Certification
The **ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)** certification is designed for beginners starting in cybersecurity.  
It validates foundational knowledge of security concepts, principles, and best practices.  

It is also a pathway toward advanced ISC2 certifications like CISSP, SSCP, and CCSP.

---

## ✅ Exam Domains
The exam covers 5 knowledge domains:

1. **Security Principles (26%)**  
   - CIA triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability)  
   - Security controls and frameworks  
   - Risk management basics  

2. **Business Continuity, Disaster Recovery, and Incident Response Concepts (10%)**  
   - BCP and DR planning basics  
   - Incident response phases  
   - Recovery objectives (RPO, RTO)  

3. **Access Controls Concepts (22%)**  
   - Authentication, authorization, and accounting (AAA)  
   - Identity management basics  
   - Least privilege and separation of duties  

4. **Network Security (24%)**  
   - TCP/IP basics, ports, and protocols  
   - Firewalls, IDS/IPS, VPNs  
   - Secure network architecture  

5. **Security Operations (18%)**  
   - Monitoring, logging, and SIEM basics  
   - Vulnerability management  
   - Malware and common attack types  
   - Policies, procedures, and training
