# ITIL v4 Foundation Certification Notes

Welcome to my **ITIL v4 Foundation Notes Repository** 📘.  
This folder is dedicated to documenting my preparation for the **ITIL v4 Foundation certification**.  

The purpose is to maintain structured notes, key concepts, and references for IT Service Management (ITSM) based on the ITIL v4 framework.

---

## 📘 About the Certification
The **ITIL v4 Foundation** certification introduces the key concepts, practices, and guiding principles of modern IT Service Management.  
It focuses on aligning IT services with business value delivery through a holistic, flexible, and agile framework.

---

## ✅ Exam Topics
- **Key Concepts of Service Management**  
  - Value creation and service relationships  
  - Organizations, service providers, and consumers  

- **The Four Dimensions of Service Management**  
  - Organizations and people  
  - Information and technology  
  - Partners and suppliers  
  - Value streams and processes  

- **The ITIL Service Value System (SVS)**  
  - Guiding principles  
  - Governance  
  - Service value chain  
  - Practices and continual improvement  

- **ITIL Guiding Principles**  
  - Focus on value  
  - Start where you are  
  - Progress iteratively with feedback  
  - Collaborate and promote visibility  
  - Think and work holistically  
  - Keep it simple and practical  
  - Optimize and automate  

- **ITIL Practices (selected in scope for Foundation)**  
  - Incident management  
  - Problem management  
  - Change enablement  
  - Service desk  
  - Continual improvement  
  - Information security management  
  - Relationship management
