# Exposure Management Certification

This repository contains my notes and progress from completing the **[Exposure Management Certification](https://xm-cyber.thinkific.com/courses/exposure-management-certification)** by XM Cyber.

---

## 📘 Course Overview

The course provides a comprehensive understanding of **Cybersecurity Exposure Management**, equipping professionals to assess, prioritize, and mitigate cyber exposure effectively.

- **Duration:** ~5 hours  
- **Format:** 5 lessons with video content and quizzes  
- **Cost:** Free  
- **Platform:** [XM Cyber Thinkific](https://xm-cyber.thinkific.com/courses/exposure-management-certification)

---

## 🎯 Who Should Enroll

Ideal for cybersecurity professionals in roles such as:

- Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) Analysts  
- Security Operations Center (SOC) Analysts  
- Penetration Testers  
- Chief Information Security Officers (CISOs)
