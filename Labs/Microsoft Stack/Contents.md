## ✅ Covered Areas
These are the areas I will cover through labs (aligned with SC-200 exam objectives):

- **Microsoft Defender XDR**
  - Onboarding devices (Windows, Linux, Server, VM)
  - Configuring Attack Surface Reduction (ASR) rules
  - Investigating alerts & incidents
- **Microsoft Sentinel**
  - Connecting data sources
  - Building analytics rules
  - Hunting queries (KQL)
  - Incident management
- **Microsoft Defender for Cloud**
  - Security recommendations
  - Just-in-Time (JIT) VM access
  - Compliance policies
- **Microsoft Defender for Identity**
  - Monitoring Active Directory signals
  - Detecting lateral movement
- **UEBA (User & Entity Behavior Analytics)**
  - Investigating risky sign-ins
  - Identifying insider threats
