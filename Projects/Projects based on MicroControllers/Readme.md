# 🔌 Projects Based on Microcontrollers

This section contains my **embedded systems and robotics projects**, developed during my B.Tech and extended for learning.  
These projects use **microcontrollers** such as **AT89C51 (8051 family), Arduino, and ARM-based boards**, along with sensor integration.

---

## 📑 Projects

### 1. [Fire and Metal Detection Robot (AT89C51)](./Fire%20and%20Metal%20Detection%20Robot%20(AT89C51)/)
- Built a robotic vehicle capable of detecting **fire and metal objects/mines**.  
- Used **AT89C51 microcontroller**, flame sensor, 555-timer-based metal detection circuit, LCD, buzzer, and DC motors.  
- Simulated in **Proteus**, programmed in **Embedded C (Keil IDE)**.  

---

## 🔧 About Microcontroller-Based Projects
- Microcontrollers are compact integrated circuits that act as the **brains of embedded systems**.  
- They combine a processor, memory, and peripherals for **real-time control**.  
- Applications range from **robots and IoT devices** to **industrial automation and defense**.  

These projects helped me develop strong fundamentals in **electronics, embedded programming, and system integration**.
